How PIGGY works

Launch status: The game is not open yet. These instructions describe its planned use.

Feed the pig

Pay SOL to buy round keys. Rounds start at five minutes. Each key adds 30 seconds, up to a ten-minute remaining-time cap. A purchase takes the lead; later purchases take it from you without removing your keys.

Hold keys

Your keys share the key-reward allocation from feeds. More keys mean a larger share. No new feeds means no new feed-funded rewards.

Be last at zero

The final eligible feeder wins 48% of the remaining round pool. Other allocations are separate.

Hold $PIGGY

The separate token qualifies for funded, published SOL rewards without staking. The new token mint has not yet been supplied.

Claiming after launch

Connect the eligible wallet, open Rewards, choose Game rewards or $PIGGY rewards, review fees and approve.
