PIGGY — Asset credits

PIGGY’s interface, model, pig logo and rendering dependencies have separate sources and licences.

Konterball interface and screen transitions

Adapted from Konterball, WILD's Google WebVR experiment. Source revision: 1b1a54aec27f36c4337f4206eb35938f530e847a. Selected interface styles, the staggered screen-transition sequence and original back-arrow SVG are reused or adapted under the MIT licence.

Changes include PIGGY game controls, Web Animations transitions, free font replacements and portrait layout. The original commissioned music and proprietary font files are not included.

Piggy Bank Glass

Piggy Bank Glass by tomgeurts, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The source mesh is retained; runtime changes adjust its scale, placement, orientation and glass material.

The PIGGY mark and favicon adapt that same mesh: orthographic projection, merged triangle silhouettes, simplified vector outlines and white/yellow recolouring on transparent or purple backgrounds. These derived logo assets retain the source model's CC BY 4.0 attribution.

Rendering and physics

  • Poimandres Drei-vanilla: MeshTransmissionMaterial shader by N8Programs and the official backside/front capture setup. MIT notice.
  • Poimandres Aquarium: adapted studio-light placement. No Aquarium model assets are included. MIT notice.
  • Rapier3D: rigid-body coin simulation. Apache 2.0 licence.

Solana coin emblem

The coin faces use the exact path from the official Solana Foundation logomark, retained as an unchanged reference SVG. Its three bars are extruded into both faces of the procedural gold coins, including the jackpot animation. Brand reference.
