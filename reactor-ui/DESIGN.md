# PIGGY — Feed Piggy. Take the crown.

PIGGY makes the pig the game: feed SOL, grow your cut, take the crown and compete for the pot at zero. The pig silhouette is the brand mark, derived from the same attributed mesh as the central prize bank. KONTERBALL supplies the adapted interface source. The upstream checkout is `research/konterball-upstream` at commit `1b1a54aec27f36c4337f4206eb35938f530e847a`. The source is MIT licensed; the original notice and project attribution are retained with the other asset credits.

`src/konterball-ui.css` maps the original source styling to the PIGGY controls. Its header comment records the individual source files. Exact purple #2f2475, pink #ffa3b0, yellow #f9fc56 and white come from `src/stylesheets/base/_vars.scss`. The 2px white rectangular buttons, uppercase labels, .08em tracking and transparent backgrounds adapt `.btn` in `base/_layout.scss`. The flat corner actions follow the original About and mute controls. The full viewport field, centered world and bottom action positions adapt `screens/_choose-mode-screen.scss`. Large white score typography follows the room-code and in-world score treatments. Information screens adapt the original full-viewport About screen: a flat purple field, centered content with internal overflow only when needed, and the source back-arrow control in the upper-left corner. They are native dialogs for focus handling, but are not bordered popup cards.

KONTERBALL's original Antique Olive and Futura fonts are replaced with the freely licensed Archivo Black and Jost Italic. Archivo Black carries the wordmark and result headings. Thin white Jost numerals carry the jackpot and countdown, matching the source game's unboxed Futura score treatment; Jost Italic carries actions and explanatory text. The source's unusual colour, typography and controls remain the visible foundation. There are no rounded glass panels, gradient navigation cards, generic dashboard surfaces or neon framing rings.

The floating pig remains the physical prize bank in the central scene, flanked by the jackpot and timer as unboxed white scores. The flat purple field and outlined controls carry the KONTERBALL interface treatment. A compact leader line sits above the object. The outlined **Feed Piggy X SOL** action shows the actual payment below the object. **Feed size** uses 1× / 5× / 10×; **Your cut** expresses the player's percentage of the reward pool instead of foregrounding a count or inventing a participation-unit brand. Gold coins represent SOL being paid into Piggy. Feed size, cost and time remain compact; the reward cut and payout mode explain the resulting position. Technical rules and reward details retain `keys`, the original Fomo3D accounting term. Rewards and wallet controls remain accessible in the top chrome. Existing DOM IDs, rules and accounting remain intact.

Portrait support is a deliberate extension. The original source's rotate-phone interruption is not used. The layout targets one fixed viewport, including 390 × 844 and 360 × 640, with a smaller scoreboard above the central world and a thumb-accessible action below it. The primary action remains at least 49px tall; the quantity row is 48px tall, with 44px increment / decrement targets. Quantity presets are hidden on phones so the essential controls retain their touch area. Header actions, payout-mode control and simulation toggle also retain 44px targets. Native dialogs may scroll internally, while the game page never scrolls. Reduced motion removes travel and timing animations without hiding state changes.

Purchase feedback is tied to the booked demo result: falling coins, pot change, time extension, crown change and the actual key-reward credit from that feed. A persistent Game SOL to claim meter shows earned, unclaimed game SOL; $PIGGY-holder SOL remains separate. **Your cut** previews the current → after-feed percentage of the mode-dependent key-reward pool, not a fixed percentage of the whole payment. A closing estimate is labelled separately from claimable SOL. The leader slot doubles as a short event message. NPC activity remains labelled and can be paused.

Piggy bursts only when the round settles at zero. Actual mesh triangles form the fragments and current coin poses seed the spill; the bounded 3.6-second effect lifts coins into a shining gold fan. Reduced motion uses a static separated pig and lifted coins without flight or shake. A new round restores the intact bank and normal coins. This is visual feedback and does not affect accounting.

The authored **Piggy Bank Glass** model by tomgeurts remains licensed CC BY 4.0 and bundled as `public/models/piggy-bank-glass.glb`. The PIGGY logo and favicon are simplified vector projections of that model, with source and modification credit retained. Their attribution is independent of KONTERBALL's MIT source licence.

A 1× feed takes the crown; larger feeds add time and increase the feeder's cut of the key-reward pool. Another feeder can take the crown while earlier participants keep their position and earned SOL. The last feeder when the clock reaches zero wins the displayed pot. Wallet-held $PIGGY receives its allocated SOL separately, without staking. The default interface reads a repaired Rust V2 program on a dedicated local Solana validator and a local test $PIGGY mint. A separate browser simulation remains at `?demo=1`; neither mode specifies a token market price or fixed yield.

Feed pricing now follows the original Solana curve and its integer rounding, using opening rollover plus gross contributions. The browser independently implements that arithmetic and quotes the minimum lamport payment for the selected whole-key count. SOL amounts retain up to nine decimal places so a small feed never becomes a misleading zero-cost label. At zero curve funding, the first whole key costs 0.000075001 SOL. The four payout modes, event-based reward bookkeeping and 30-second-per-key timer remain separate from that pricing change; the default local interface reads the repaired Rust V2 program; the separate browser concept remains at `?demo=1`.

The settlement direction is a gold jackpot: 3.6 seconds of upward coin motion into a suspended fan, per-coin glints, a single warm flash and a gold payout state. Coins do not fall into a wreckage pile. A museum-style overhead spotlight and broad front bounce reveal the existing glass and stamped coin surfaces. No perpetual animation is added.
